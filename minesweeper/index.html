Minesweeper Help

Minesweeper

Find every mine without uncovering one. The numbers tell you how many mines touch a square.

How to play

  • Left-click a square to uncover it.
  • Right-click to place a flag on a suspected mine. Right-click again for a question mark (when Marks is enabled).
  • Double-click a number, or click it with both mouse buttons (or middle-click), to open all of its neighbours once the right number of flags are placed.
  • Click the smiley face to start a new game.

Winning

Uncover every safe square to win. Your fastest times for Beginner, Intermediate and Expert are saved in your browser and can be viewed under Game › Best Times.

Keyboard

F2New game
F1Open this help window
EscClose menus and dialogs

Tips

  • Your first click is always safe. The board is generated after you click.
  • A number shows how many mines are in the eight surrounding squares.
  • Use flags to keep track of mines, then chord around numbers to clear space quickly.
