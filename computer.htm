We have recently done some interesting EMI/EMC measurements in our laboratory. A very crude way of measuring and observing the effect of electromagnetic and static field is to probe with ac voltmeter. And here is some measured values:

Everything including mains off  0.00 V everywhere.
All equipments off, and mains on  2.3 V 1m away from the printer SMPS and 2' away from computer SMPS. 
Computer SMPS off, printer SMPS on 1.8 V. (probe is in the same place)
CPU and printer turned on 3.6 V (probe is in the same place)
CPU, printer, and Monitor on 5.7 V (without changing probe position)   

The same measurement have been carried out for other computers to and for everything switched on, upto 7 V was measured. 

So always try to switch off the monitor when you are not working for some time and obviously before closing the lab switch off everything. It is not a question of computer health but our health.
