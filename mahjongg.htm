🀄 The goal

Remove every tile from the turtle-shaped board. Click two free tiles that match: identical tiles, any flower with any flower, or any season with any season.

A tile is free when no tile sits on top of it and at least one long side (left or right) is open. Free tiles are bright; blocked tiles are dimmed.

🎴 The tile set

  • 3 suits × 9 ranks × 4: circles, bamboo, characters
  • 4 winds × 4: 東 南 西 北
  • 3 dragons × 4: 中 發 白
  • 4 flowers (梅 蘭 菊 竹) and 4 seasons (春 夏 秋 冬) — each matches within its group

144 tiles in 72 pairs.

⌨️ Controls & help

  • H hint • U undo • S shuffle • P pause • N new game
  • Every deal is generated from a legal solution, so a winning line always exists.
  • Hints cost 100 points; shuffles cost 200. Undo returns the points from that pair.
  • If you paint yourself into a corner, shuffle keeps the remaining board solvable.

🐧 About the original

Mahjongg has shipped with Linux desktops for decades — as xgmmahjongg, then GNOME Mahjongg and KDE's KMahjongg — using the classic 144-tile turtle layout recreated here.

This version keeps the familiar rules and adds browser conveniences: blocked-tile shading, hints, undo, auto-solvable shuffles and saved best scores.
