About Me 

I was born in the historic place Chandannagore, a city 30 km away from Howrah. I received BE (Electronics) and ME (Tele-Communication and Digital Systems and Instrumentation) degree in 1997 and 1999 respectively  from Bengal Engineering College (Deemed University). Currently I am doing PhD in Microwave Planar Circuits.

Apart from academic interest, my hobby includes aquarium, gardening, electronics projects and computer. I use to listen all types of music including Indian-foreign pop-rock-folk-tabla-piano.

I used to play Foot-ball, Cricket, Badminton, Table tennis but now a days quite reluctant. Now strongly dislike Cricket specially watching matches in TV. 

Non-vegetarian foods like egg-roll, moglaai-paratha, chicken cutlet, Fish fry or vegetarian dishes like dal-rice-begun bhaja, luchii alur dam make me nostalgic. 

I am totally against war and just cannot tolerate killing/torturing people in the name of nation, religion or race.
