⚡ Microstrip Line Synthesis & Analysis

Uses Wheeler / Hammerstad & Jensen closed-form models with Newton-Raphson precision convergence:

  • For $W/h \le 2$: Fringe fields dominate, $\varepsilon_{eff}$ drops towards $(\varepsilon_r+1)/2$.
  • For $W/h > 2$: Parallel-plate TEM capacitance dominates. Standard $50\,\Omega$ trace on $1.6\text{ mm}$ FR4 yields $W \approx 3.08\text{ mm}$ ($W/h \approx 1.93$).
  • Quarter-Wave ($\lambda/4$ = 90°): The foundation for impedance matching transformers, branch-line couplers, and DC blocking stubs.

📡 Stripline & Coaxial Geometries

  • Stripline: Pure TEM propagation with zero dispersion ($\varepsilon_{eff} = \varepsilon_r$). Excellent electromagnetic shielding with zero radiation losses.
  • Coaxial Lines: Pure TEM mode below the $TE_{11}$ waveguide cutoff ($f_c \approx 2c / (\pi \sqrt{\varepsilon_r}(D+d))$). The classic $50\,\Omega$ standard is an optimal balance between power handling ($30\,\Omega$) and attenuation ($77\,\Omega$).

📐 Coupled Microstrip Lines (Filters & Couplers)

Solves coupled even ($Z_{0e}$) and odd ($Z_{0o}$) modes via Akhtarzad's equivalent single-line transformation:

  • Even Mode ($Z_{0e}$): Equal voltages on both strips, magnetic wall between them ($Z_{0e} > Z_0$).
  • Odd Mode ($Z_{0o}$): Opposite voltages on strips, electric wall between them ($Z_{0o} < Z_0$).
  • Matching Condition: $Z_0 = \sqrt{Z_{0e} \cdot Z_{0o}}$.
